## 关于我 / About Me

我是岳磊，南京师范大学心理学在读博士生，目前主要研究方向是心理学中的**开放科学（open science）**与**元科学（metascience）**。我于 2015 年进入心理学，硕士阶段在华中师范大学研究**物质主义价值观**；从 2021 年起，我的研究兴趣逐渐转向心理学的研究方法及实践，当前我正在关注心理学人类样本的相关内容。

I am Yue Lei, a PhD student in Psychology at Nanjing Normal University. My primary research interests lie in **open science** and **metascience** within psychology. I embarked on my journey in psychology in 2015. During my master’s studies at Central China Normal University, I researched **materialism values**. Since 2021, my research focus has gradually shifted toward research methodology and research practices in psychology. My current work centers on human participant samples in psychological research.
